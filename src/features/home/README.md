# Home Feature

## Overview

The Home feature acts as the main storefront landing experience for GradFlowers, guiding visitors through curated commencement collections, live alma mater ribbon customization, bestselling bouquets, and ceremony delivery guarantees.

## Components

- `HeroSection.tsx`: Editorial split hero with interactive arrangement preview switcher, simulated gold foil ribbon overlay, live social proof stats, and quick CTA buttons.
- `FeaturedBouquetsSection.tsx`: Displays top-rated commencement bestseller arrangements with full loading, empty, error, and success states.
- `CategoryShowcase.tsx`: Visual cards for Sunflowers, Roses, Leis, and Corsages with floral symbolism tags.
- `SchoolColorsMatcher.tsx`: Interactive university color studio with live 24K gold foil ribbon inscription preview and recommended bouquet pairing.
- `CeremonyProcessSection.tsx`: Three-step campus gate delivery workflow and verified graduate & family testimonials.
- `FeaturesHighlight.tsx`: Benefits including commemorative ribbons and venue handoffs.
- `CeremonyFaq.tsx`: Animated frequently asked questions regarding campus delivery and freshness, plus a pre-order promotional banner.
- `pages/HomePage.tsx`: Route composition container.
